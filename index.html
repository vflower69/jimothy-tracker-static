Seattle’s Little Wanderer

Jimothy the raccoon is a uniquely proportioned Seattle local, often spotted around the Ballard Locks. His short neck, rounded torso, and long legs caught public attention after a video of him roaming outdoors went viral in mid‑July 2026. Since then, neighbors have reported frequent sightings of Jimothy exploring the Ballard Locks area and its surrounding green spaces.

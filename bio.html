Seattle’s Little Wanderer

Jimothy the raccoon is a uniquely proportioned Seattle local, often spotted around Ballard and its surrounding green spaces. His short neck, rounded torso, and long legs caught public attention after a video of him roaming outdoors went viral in mid‑July 2026.

Since then, Jimothy has become a beloved figure in the community. Residents frequently share sightings, photos, and stories of his adventures. He’s known for his curiosity, gentle demeanor, and knack for appearing in unexpected places — from backyard gardens to waterfront parks.

The Jimothy Tracker was created to celebrate this local legend and help the community follow his travels. Every sighting helps build a clearer picture of Jimothy’s favorite routes, seasonal habits, and interactions with Seattle’s urban wildlife.

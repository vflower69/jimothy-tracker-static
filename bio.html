Urban Legend · Ballard, Seattle

Seattle’s Little Wanderer

Jimothy the raccoon isn’t just a Seattle local — he’s a full-blown cultural phenomenon. Known for his tiny torso and comically long legs, Jimothy first captured public attention when a mid-July 2026 video of his fence-balancing antics went viral and left the internet collectively asking what kind of raccoon physics they were witnessing.

Long before the memes, Jimothy was already a familiar face to Ballard residents. Locals watched him grow from a clumsy youngster into a confident explorer who treated backyard fences like runways. Veterinarians confirmed his proportions stem from a congenital spinal condition — unusual, but not harmful — giving him his signature silhouette and inspiring a thousand fan posts.

In one of the most surreal and delightful moments of his rise to fame, the University of Washington officially joined the fun by awarding “Dr. Jimothy” an honorary degree in a widely shared social media post, recognizing his “contributions to community joy, ecological curiosity, and general raccoon excellence.”

The Jimothy Tracker was created to celebrate this beloved urban character and help the community follow his travels. Jimothy isn’t just a raccoon — he’s a vibe, a movement, and a tiny-torsoed ambassador of chaotic good.

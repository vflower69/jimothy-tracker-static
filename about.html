Why Jimothy Tracker Exists

JimothyTracker.org is a community wildlife awareness project. It was created to help the community better understand Jimothy’s natural movement patterns, habitat preferences, and seasonal behavior. By collecting sightings, we build a clearer picture of where Jimothy spends his time and how he interacts with Seattle’s urban environment.

This information can be genuinely useful for wildlife researchers, urban ecology students, and official wildlife management teams. Community‑driven data helps identify raccoon travel corridors, food sources, and environmental pressures — all without disturbing Jimothy or altering his behavior.

Respecting Jimothy

Jimothy is a wild animal. He is not a tourist attraction, mascot, or scavenger hunt objective. The purpose of this tracker is understanding — not pursuit.

The tracking data must NOT be used for:

  • Hunting Jimothy
  • Chasing or attempting to approach him
  • Stalking him across neighborhoods
  • Trying to lure him out or interfere with his routine
  • Posting intentionally false sightings (e.g., “Jimothy spotted in Korea”)

Reports that are clearly unrealistic or harmful may be removed to protect the integrity of the data and Jimothy’s safety.

How You Can Help

If you spot Jimothy, feel free to submit a sighting — responsibly. Provide accurate location details, avoid exaggeration, and always respect his space. Every honest report helps build a better understanding of his habitat and supports ethical wildlife awareness.

About Jimothy Tracker

Our Purpose

Jimothy Tracker was created to help the community better understand and appreciate Jimothy — a beloved local raccoon whose adventures have captured the hearts of many. By collecting sighting reports, we aim to build a clearer picture of his habitat, movement patterns, and living environment.

This information can be valuable not only for curious neighbors, but also for wildlife researchers and official wildlife management teams who monitor urban raccoon populations and their interactions with human environments.

Community Responsibility

Jimothy is a wild animal, not a mascot or a tourist attraction. The data shared on this site is intended for understanding his habitat — not for chasing, disturbing, or attempting to capture him.

Please do not use Jimothy Tracker for:
  • Hunting Jimothy
  • Chasing or harassing him
  • Attempting to lure him out
  • Stalking him across neighborhoods

Reports of sightings outside realistic ranges (e.g., “Jimothy spotted in Korea”) may be removed to keep the data accurate and prevent misuse.

How You Can Help

If you spot Jimothy, feel free to submit a report — but please do so responsibly. Provide accurate location information, avoid exaggeration, and respect his space. Together, we can maintain a safe, informative, and fun community resource.

Thank you for helping protect Jimothy and supporting ethical wildlife awareness.
